About ltc.tx.taxi

ltc.tx.taxi is the Litecoin explorer in the tx.taxi multi-chain family. It preserves Mempool's proven interface for inspecting blocks, transactions, addresses, mining activity, fee estimates, and the live Litecoin mempool while using a Litecoin-specific tx.taxi identity.

Litecoin-native data

Values are denominated in LTC, litoshis, or the selected fiat currency. Fee rates remain lit/vB, transaction sizes remain vB or weight units, and all block, address, and mining concepts shown by the interface retain their Litecoin meaning.

MWEB coverage

This explorer shows transparent-chain transactions and indexed addresses. Private MWEB amounts and recipient history are not publicly observable. HogEx transactions connect the extension block to the transparent chain.

Infrastructure

The explorer reads public Litecoin network data through its configured backend. Explorer results are informational and can differ from another node's view of the mempool. Always verify payment details in your wallet and use your own node when independent verification matters.

Built on open source

This explorer builds on the Mempool Open Source Project. See third-party attribution and contact the maintainer for source and license information.
